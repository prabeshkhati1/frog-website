Froggy Facts

🏆

Champion jumpers! A frog's back legs can launch it up to 20 times its own body length.

👀

Their eyes bulge to swallow their food — they push food down with their eyeballs. Yes, really!

🫁

Frogs breathe through their skin too — some can even hibernate underwater all winter.

🔊

Gulp, gulp, gulp — frogs swallow with their eyes closed and their eardrums on the outside of their heads.

❄️

The frozen wood frog can have 65% of its body freeze solid — then hop away in the spring!

🌍

There are about 7,000 species of frogs and toads hopping across every continent except Antarctica.
